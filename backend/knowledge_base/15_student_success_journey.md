# Student Success Journey & Graduation

MathPath guides students through a proven 5-step journey from their very first foundation class all the way to graduation.

## How MathPath Works (The 5-Step Process)
1. **Learn:** Structured lessons with expert certified instructors teaching core abacus techniques.
2. **Practise:** Daily 10-minute guided practice sessions on the MathPath App.
3. **Unlock:** Advance to new levels and unlock new lessons as skills are mastered.
4. **Assess:** Regular assessments and mock practice to track mastery.
5. **Graduate:** Celebrate achievements at every level and move up the curriculum.

## The Student Success Journey Timeline
As students progress through the 9-level structured curriculum, they evolve through five distinct stages of mastery:
- **Foundation:** Building strong basics with the Abacus.
- **Fluency:** Developing speed, accuracy, and calculation confidence.
- **Visualisation:** Enhancing mental strength, picture memory, and focus.
- **Advanced Skills:** Solving complex school-style problems with ease.
- **Graduation:** Becoming confident, capable, and future-ready.

## Graduation Wall & Ceremonies
MathPath believes in celebrating success. We host Graduation Ceremonies where students receive certificates and recognition for their hard work. 
- **Milestone Celebrations:** Every level completed is a milestone celebrated.
- **Annual Competitions:** MathPath conducts annual competitions (eligible from Level 2 onwards). It is a full-day event with result declarations and prize distributions on the same day.
- **The Graduation Wall:** A prestigious showcase of our graduates, celebrating their dedication, consistency, and mathematical excellence.

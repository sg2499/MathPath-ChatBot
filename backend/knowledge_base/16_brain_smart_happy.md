# Brain Smart Happy (Whole-Child Learning)

MathPath is not just about calculation; it is about holistic cognitive development. The **Brain Smart Happy** module is our Whole-Child Learning initiative.

## What is Brain Smart Happy?
It is a specialized segment of our curriculum designed to improve focus, memory, logic, and creativity in children. Every class includes a 30-minute Brain Smart Happy session so children can learn maths in a fun, engaging, and holistic way.

## Key Features
- **Memory, Attention, and Reasoning Sessions:** Targeted exercises that improve cognitive retention and logical thinking.
- **Movement-Based & Creative Thinking Activities:** Keeping children physically and mentally engaged, preventing learning fatigue.
- **Confidence Building:** The ultimate goal is to build confidence for both school and life.

Through Brain Smart Happy, MathPath ensures that children develop the mental strength and focus required not just to solve math problems, but to tackle academic and real-world challenges with a positive mindset.

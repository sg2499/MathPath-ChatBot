# MathPath Policies, Terms, and Pricing

## Pricing Policy
MathPath's pricing structure is designed to be transparent and affordable for all students.
The fee structure is as follows:
- **Registration Amount:** ₹1650 (One Time, required at the time of admission)
- **Tuition Fees:** ₹1100 (Monthly, for academic instruction)
- **Content Charges:** Level Specific (Charged at the start of every new level)

*Note: The institute reserves the right to revise the pricing structure when necessary. Any changes will be communicated in advance.*

## Refund and Cancellation Policy
- Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of payment.
- Refunds will be processed only in cases of duplicate payment, technical errors, or non-delivery of service.
- No refunds will be provided after service activation or course access.
- Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.
- For refund requests, parents must contact MathPath at bgmenterprisekol@gmail.com.

## Shipping and Delivery Policy
MathPath primarily offers digital educational services. No physical product is shipped.
- Access credentials, learning materials, and services are delivered electronically via email or the online portal (MathPath App).
- The delivery timeline is within 24 hours of successful payment.
- In case of delayed delivery, parents should contact MathPath immediately.

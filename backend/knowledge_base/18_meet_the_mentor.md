# Meet The Mentor: Ashalatha Gupta

Ashalatha Gupta is the founder, mentor, and learning guide behind MathPath. Her work focuses on helping children overcome fear of numbers and develop a confident, joyful relationship with mathematics.

## Experience
With years of experience in guiding young learners through abacus, arithmetic development, structured practice, and confidence building, she has shaped MathPath as a thoughtful learning journey rather than a rushed calculation course.

## Philosophy
Her philosophy is simple: every child can learn mathematics when the method is clear, the pace is right, and encouragement is consistent.

## Student Impact
Students under her guidance learn to calculate better, think more clearly, practise regularly, and believe in their own ability. Parents value the structured approach, personal attention, and visible progress.

At MathPath, our goal is not only to teach mathematics. Our goal is to help every child believe, "I can do this." — Ashalatha Gupta
